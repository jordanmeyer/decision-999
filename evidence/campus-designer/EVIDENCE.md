# Campus Designer evidence

The listing cites the October 2, 2026 independent review of three fictional pages: an AI/leadership event, a research initiative and a student organization. All three passed after one or two correction rounds. Five implementation defects were found and fixed: two enlarged-layout event issues, one research text overflow, and two student-organization labeling/layout issues. This is iterative artifact review, not a first-attempt success rate or accessibility certification.
