The conversation

What does good leadership look like when the tools keep changing?

AI invites organizations to rethink how work gets done. It also brings questions that technology alone cannot answer: Who decides? Who benefits? And who is accountable?

This imagined symposium makes room for those questions through discussion, case studies, and hands-on reflection. The program below is entirely illustrative.

01 / The day

A program built around the decisions ahead.

Sample agenda
All times Eastern Time

  1. 9:00 a.m.

    Opening conversation

    Beyond the technology: the leadership question

    Frame the choices that emerge when AI becomes part of everyday organizational work.

  2. 10:15 a.m.

    Case discussion

    Who owns the decision?

    Examine an invented hiring scenario and consider where human judgment, oversight, and accountability belong.

  3. 11:30 a.m.

    Working session

    From principles to everyday practice

    Sketch a responsible rollout plan, with space for the people whose work will change.

  4. 12:30 p.m.

    Break

    Lunch & open conversation

    Time to reflect, compare questions, and connect across perspectives.

  5. 1:30 p.m.

    Panel & audience discussion

    Building trust through change

    Explore communication, employee voice, and the limits of a purely technical solution.

  6. 3:00 p.m.

    Reflection lab · closes at 4:00 p.m.

    The next question you’ll take with you

    Translate the day’s conversations into a set of questions for your own context.
