# Desk / Day — participant task

[Open the app](https://jordanmeyer.github.io/bab-example-presentation/). Reload or reset it before starting.

Allow 10 minutes. Start from the default app supplied by the facilitator. Think aloud; this reviews the app, not you. State predictions before changes or worked answers. You may use the app's guidance. Stop at the time limit and describe where you reached.

1. Explain the opening recommendation, operating result and funding request.
2. Predict what the Lower 20,000 demand case recommends. Apply it and explain which policy gate matters.
3. Enter price 180, unit cost 108, quantity 1,450 and fixed cost 100,000. Compare the full and pilot alternatives. Explain the policy's choice and whether you would make the same choice.
4. Leave price blank and explain which values the slides retain. Recover, use Read all slides and return to slide view without losing your valid scenario.
5. Copy the policy recommendation. Explain how that record differs from your own decision. Name something a pilot could teach and a later choice that evidence could change.
