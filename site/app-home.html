The process

From problem to published app

  1. Starts with a real problem

    Choose a task you know firsthand. Identify its users, their decisions, and concrete examples of a useful result.

  2. Built through conversation

    Describe the application in plain language. The agent handles implementation while you review the interface, calculations and behavior.

  3. Tested and reviewed

    Check known answers, realistic cases, keyboard use and narrow screens. Have the app reviewed and revise it before publication.

  4. Published with its limits

    Publish the working app and GitHub source with its assumptions, testing evidence and remaining limitations. Use only public or synthetic data.
