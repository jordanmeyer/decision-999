# Novice walkthrough packet — 12 apps

Prepared October 9, 2026. **No participant sessions have been conducted or claimed by this packet.** ALL-16, PROC-12 and SALES-13 remain open until actual observations, resulting revisions and any needed follow-up are recorded. Model tests, agent interaction checks and this preparation do not establish learning outcomes.

Give a participant only their linked task sheet. Keep [facilitator answers and protocol](FACILITATOR.md) private during the attempt. Copy the [blank observation form](OBSERVATION-FORM.md) for each app/session. Participants may use the app's visible guidance, but must state a prediction before opening a worked answer; record when guidance was used. This reviews the app, not the participant.

## Evidence gates

| Checklist item | Prepared task coverage | What remains to be observed |
|---|---|---|
| ALL-16, each of 12 apps | Each sheet asks for default interpretation, a prediction before a change, invalid-input recovery and a limitation. | An actual novice's uncoached attempt, confusion, revisions and targeted follow-up. Independently completed, assisted and unfinished steps stay distinct. |
| PROC-12 | Process steps 3–4: add/connect, branch shares, capacity, named-loop recovery, export and reimport. | A novice's complete cycle. Record whether pointer or keyboard forms were used; verify the equivalent keyboard-form cycle separately if it was not the observed route. Preparation alone verifies neither route. |
| SALES-13 | Sales steps 2–5: download template, add row, import, break date, verify retained identity, correct/reimport, reset filters/errors. | The actual file workflow performed by the novice. A prebuilt fixture, facilitator performing the import, or model test does not substitute. |

Actual screen-reader evaluation is separate: record the assistive technology, spoken output, focus and complete task observations under its own protocol. DOM roles, keyboard use and novice participation alone do not establish a screen-reader pass. Likewise, these sessions do not replace the required 320 px and 200% text browser checks.

## Preparation sources and scope

The nine existing [per-app scripts](../) were read and condensed into answer-free sheets; their earlier files and failure records remain intact. This packet supersedes their inconsistent timing and mixed participant/facilitator presentation for these sessions. The original pricing, inventory and sales tasks were derived from their current `app/learn.html`, models and controls under `/private/tmp/bab-checklist-originals-2026-10-09/`. Sales uses the current 12-row template, not the historical three-row default. Historical local preview ports remain in developer evidence; participant links above use the verified public builds.

No optional extension is required to complete a timed module. Demand-response optimization, repeated-run inventory estimation, extra simulator boundary cases and additional board questions remain later teaching work. Do not treat omission from this bounded novice session as removal of an app's separate checklist obligation.
