# Launch Ledger — current plan

Original opening request: “We're coordinating a small product launch with work across research, design, procurement and go-to-market. I'd like an interactive roadmap that exposes dependencies, shows what a delay changes, and makes the gap between scheduled dates and actual resource feasibility clear. We need a useful initial plan, editable dates, milestones and a readable task view.” The initial simulated planning is retained in PLANNING-CONVERSATION.md. On October 9 the app owner authorized bringing the live example up to revised guidance, including resources and milestones that the original implementation omitted.

## Model and first-load lesson

Whole calendar days, inclusive start and exclusive end; no weekend/holiday adjustment. Start Nov 2, 2026; promise Dec 1, 2026. Work tasks: validation5 days; packaging8 after validation; supplier10 after validation; pilot4 after packaging+supplier; safety7 after pilot; sales6 after packaging; launch preparation1 after safety+sales. Milestones Design sign-off and Ready to launch are zero-duration events after packaging and launch respectively. Their successors may start on the same day. All tasks/events must finish. The baseline finishes Nov29 with2 days promise buffer; packaging+5 or safety+3 gives Dec2,1 day late. Promise changes never reschedule work. Zero float uses earliest project completion, not promise.

Resource teams Research/Design/Launch team each have1 person/day available, constant across calendar days. Validation needs1 Research; packaging1 Design. Launch team assignments: supplier0.7, pilot1, safety0.5, sales0.6, launch1 people/day. Milestones use none. Demand adds over overlapping [start,end) days. The default overload is1.3 on Nov15–16 (supplier+sales),1.6 on Nov17–20 (pilot+sales):6 days, peak1.6. Capacity1.6 clears overload with identical dates. This is a capacity diagnostic, not automatic leveling, a probabilistic forecast, cost estimate or safety assessment. Dependency readiness is not a feasible commitment while workload exceeds capacity.

## Interface and edits

Persistent Frappe Gantt1.2.2 shows safe textContent task names, zero-width milestone anchors replaced with diamonds, and a labeled promised-date line. Width/padding cover both schedule and commitment. Accessible exact task table has readable dates and resource assumptions. Native forms include all project, task, milestone and team-capacity edits. Changes apply together because partially typed dates/dependencies may temporarily invalidate the plan; pending notice explicitly says results show the last applied plan. Task/chart selectors are unavailable until Apply all edits or a scenario reset. Errors pause calculations; restore last valid recovers. Baseline/import semantics remain explicit and transient. Fonts are local licensed EB Garamond400 and Open Sans400/600 with published stacks and lining figures. Project/file settings, editor and method details use disclosure to shorten first load. Native pickers follow browser locale; all authored dates use Nov29,2026 style.
