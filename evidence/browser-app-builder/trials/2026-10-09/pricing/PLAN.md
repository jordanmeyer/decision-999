# Pricing scenario calculator — agreed simulated plan

An MBA user compares a single product in one period. Quantity is a sales assumption; the calculator does not predict demand. The smallest useful version accepts price per unit, variable unit cost, fixed period costs, and quantity, then shows revenue, variable costs, contribution per unit, profit and minimum whole-unit break-even.

USD inputs accept ordinary nonnegative decimals through two places, including .30, with a $1,000,000 cap each. Quantity accepts nonnegative whole numbers through 1,000,000. Leading/trailing whitespace is ignored; commas, exponent notation, negatives, missing values and excess decimals are rejected with field-specific explanations. No silent rounding. Break-even may exceed the quantity-entry cap and must remain exact and visibly distinguish that condition.

Use native HTML/CSS/JavaScript only; selected library IDs: none; plain mode. BigInt cents parse decimal strings exactly. Revenue is price × quantity; variable costs are unit cost × quantity; contribution is price minus unit cost; profit is contribution × quantity minus fixed costs. With positive contribution, whole-unit break-even is fixed costs divided by contribution, rounded up using integer arithmetic. With zero fixed costs break-even is 0, including nonpositive contribution: explain that positive sales produce zero profit at zero margin or losses at negative margin. With positive fixed costs and nonpositive contribution, break-even is impossible. Exclude taxes, financing, capacity, volume discounts and multiple products. Raising price may reduce demand; warn explicitly.

The synthetic initial example is price 19.95, cost 7.40, fixed costs 1000, quantity 80: contribution 12.55; total contribution 1004; profit 4; 79 units contribute 991.45 (loss 8.55), so minimum break-even 80. Decimal edge: .30−.20=.10; fixed 100 =>1000 units and zero profit at1000. Other independent cases: 10−6=4, fixed9 =>3 units; at2 profit−1, at3 profit3. At price1/cost1/fixed5, profit always−5. At price1/cost2/fixed0, zero sales profit0, one sale loss1. At price.01/cost0/fixed1000000, break-even100000000, above editable quantity cap.

Form edits update results immediately only if all inputs are valid; invalid values clear stale results. Reset example restores all values and results. Empty/error state names invalid fields and associates field errors. Keyboard order follows inputs then reset; clear visible focus. Apply unchanged Duke navy and royal with Georgia/Arial system fonts; no university marks or claims of endorsement. Public synthetic examples only. No imports, exports, persistence, external services, analytics, accounts or forecast. No managed build or dependencies. No real deployment or source link until a repository URL is authorized. Only app/ belongs in deployment output; workflow preparation is local only.
