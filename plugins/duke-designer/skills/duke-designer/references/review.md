# Finished-artifact review

Use applicable checks; mark each pass, fail, not applicable or unverified. Record the artifact/version, sizes and viewing method. Fix defects before delivery. A rendered preview, a source review and an interaction test are different evidence.

## Brand compliance

- Institutional relationship and mark authorization are established or explicitly unresolved; university, unit, student, athletics and Health identities are not conflated.
- Official artwork, aspect ratio, buffer, the prohibition on surrounding/overlaid patterns or designs, and mandatory co-branding provisions are preserved. Check same-plane/relative-size recommendations separately. A plain contrasting field remains a skill default. Print measurements are checked only for print output.
- At least one unchanged Duke blue is used as Duke recommends; no opacity/saturation adjustment changes it. Supporting colors have purposeful secondary roles.
- Type role matches the selected family's guidance; fonts/weights actually render, and substitutions are disclosed. Ordinary text never impersonates a wordmark.
- Image provenance, usage rights and any required credits are documented. Reference-only imagery is not treated as stock.
- Recommendations and defaults remain distinguishable from mandatory rules; any exception names its scope and evidence. Never self-approve a deviation requiring institutional approval.

## Factual accuracy

- Names, dates, locations, links, prices, affiliations, research claims and data agree with supplied evidence.
- Demonstration content and uncertain details are labeled; no fabricated endorsements, membership status, approvals or outcomes.
- Important qualifications, captions and citations survived editing/export.

## Accessibility

- Actual text/background combinations meet the applicable threshold, including interaction states. Color is not the sole cue; needed control/graphic boundaries and focus are discernible.
- Heading/reading order, labels, keyboard operation, focus, image alternatives and relevant media captions are checked for the medium.
- Web text resizing, narrow-width reflow, long headlines and reduced motion are checked; document/slide export structure is checked when relevant.
- Automated findings have been assessed; do not claim complete accessibility from a scan or brand-color table.

## Design quality (judgment, not compliance)

- Purpose, hierarchy and next action are clear to the intended audience.
- Type, space, alignment, crop and density fit the content and medium; the result does not force an unrelated template.
- Inspect actual output for clipping, overflow, awkward wraps, weak contrast, missing assets and visually distracting inconsistencies.
- Make a separate simplification pass: remove redundant decoration, repeated calls to action, excess font families or unnecessary layout mechanisms when that improves comprehension.

## Delivery note

State what changed and why, what was visually/behaviorally checked, and any unresolved limitation. A useful exception report names the rule/source, affected element, reason, temporary treatment and what would resolve it. If no rendering tool was available, explicitly report “source reviewed; rendered output unverified.” Do not describe a walkthrough or code inspection as a rendered-design review.
