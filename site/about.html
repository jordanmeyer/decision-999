Why it exists

MBA students are asked what they have built with AI, and most don’t have a strong answer. The course is designed to produce one:

“At my last job I spent ten hours a week on this task. I built a plugin that cuts it to one. Here is how I tested it, where it still fails, and what a firm would need before trusting it.”

Expertise, made actionable

MBA students arrive with years of hard-won judgment from consulting, banking, healthcare, operations, and marketing: how to size a market, read a 10-K, triage a shipment exception, or spot a weak investment memo. Most of it lives in their heads.

A plugin writes that judgment down in a form an AI agent can follow: the steps, the reference material, the checks, and the points where a person should decide. Once it’s published, anyone can install it and put that expertise to work.

Four levels of plugin

Every final project reaches level 3. Level 4 is the stretch goal.

  1. Level 1

    Structured assistant

    Instructions and reference documents that make an agent reliable at one task.

    An investment-memo critic loaded with a fund’s criteria

  2. Level 2

    Analytical engine

    Data in, verified analysis out, the same way every time.

    A comps table built from 10-K filings

  3. Level 3

    Installable plugin

    Packaged so someone other than the builder can install it and rely on it.

    A triage assistant for late shipments

  4. Level 4

    Agent workflow

    Multi-step work across tools and documents, with a person approving key steps.

    A first-pass diligence agent

Proof, not just a demo

The problem and baseline

How long the task took before, and how often it went wrong.

An eval scorecard

Accuracy, consistency, and cost per run on 20 test cases with known answers.

A risk card

Where the plugin fails and what it should not be trusted with.

A workflow map

Which steps are AI, which are rules, and where a person approves.

A demo

A short recorded walkthrough of the plugin doing the work.

Free for anyone to use

Plugins that pass review are published here at the end of each term. Install them in ChatGPT, Claude, and other AI agents at no cost. Students get real users; you get methods tested by people who have done the work.

Every plugin is built with public or synthetic data. No employer, client, or patient data, ever.
