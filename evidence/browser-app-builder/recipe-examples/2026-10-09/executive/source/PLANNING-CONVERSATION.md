# Simulated planning exchange

This exchange is a deliberately simulated student conversation between the developer and the coordinating agent. It is not a real student interview or an adoption claim. Date: 2026-10-09.

**Opening brief supplied by coordinator:** “I'm the COO of a fictional specialty coffee chain. I need a board-ready executive dashboard to see which stores are healthy, where contribution is slipping, and what to investigate this month. I want to compare regions and stores, examine a meaningful operating table, and walk the board through a few decisions. This is a classroom example with synthetic data.”

**Developer:** “Simulated planning questions for executive example: (1) Should store contribution mean sales less product cost, store labor, and occupancy/other controllable operating costs, explicitly excluding headquarters, tax and debt? (2) For the board, should health combine contribution margin against a 20% target plus year-over-year comparable sales, or is there a more meaningful threshold? (3) I propose 12 mature stores in 3 fictional regions, April–September 2026, with September versus August and prior-year same month; a board agenda highlighting two deterioration cases and a high performer. Agree, and which decisions should matter: staffing productivity, purchasing/waste, or expansion?”

**Simulated student:** “Yes—call it store contribution, subtract product cost, store labor and occupancy/other controllable costs; exclude headquarters, taxes and financing. Keep margin and comparable-sales growth separate so a composite score doesn't hide tradeoffs. Use 20% as a classroom target, clearly editable/assumed rather than an industry norm. Twelve mature stores across three fictional regions and Apr–Sep2026 is useful; show Sep/Aug and same-month prior-year comparisons. Focus this board discussion on labor productivity and purchasing/waste; expansion would need capital/demand assumptions we don't have. I want to click a deteriorating store and see which cost lines changed before deciding what to investigate, without calling correlation a cause. Include full-period monthly table/filter so 72 store-month records have a real role. Independent reference for one store: $100,000 sales, $32,000 product, $28,000 labor, $15,000 occupancy => $25,000 contribution,25%; prior-year sales$90,000 =>11.111…% growth. If5,000 transactions ticket$20. Agree to this smallest useful version; record the plan and proceed.”

**Developer:** “Executive plan accepted and recorded next. I will use separate margin and comp-sales indicators, an editable 20% classroom threshold, 72 store-month rows, weighted aggregate rates, and a store expense bridge showing dollar and percentage-point changes. Product waste remains included in product cost; the app will suggest labor/product-cost investigations without claiming causal findings.
