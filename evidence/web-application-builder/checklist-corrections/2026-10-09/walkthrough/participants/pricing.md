# Pricing — participant task

[Open the app](https://jordanmeyer.github.io/bab-trial-pricing-2026-10-08/). Reload or reset it before starting.

Allow 7 minutes. Start from the default app supplied by the facilitator. Think aloud; this is a review of the app, not a test of you. State your prediction before making each requested change or opening a worked answer. You may use the app's guidance. Stop when the facilitator calls time and describe where you reached.

1. Explain the default result, contribution per unit and whole-unit cost-coverage threshold in your own words. What period and currency do the inputs describe?
2. Save the 100-unit default as your reference. Predict the result at 62 and 63 units, then try both and reconcile the difference. Return to 100 units.
3. Predict whether changing price to $22 makes the scenario preferable. Try $22 with 100 assumed units, then with 70 units. Compare both with the saved reference and explain which assumptions drive your conclusion.
4. Leave price blank, attempt calculation, explain the feedback and recover a valid scenario. How do you know whether the result matches the current fields?
5. Copy a scenario record. Explain what you would need to learn before recommending a price, and what will remain after you reload or close the app.
