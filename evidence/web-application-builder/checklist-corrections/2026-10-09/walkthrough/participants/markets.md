# Replenish — participant task

[Open the app](https://jordanmeyer.github.io/bab-example-markets/). Reload or reset it before starting.

Allow 10 minutes. Start from the default app supplied by the facilitator. Think aloud; this reviews the app, not you. State predictions before changes or worked answers. You may use the app's guidance. Stop at the time limit and describe where you reached.

1. Explain the leading state's addressable-market number and priority score. Predict whether increasing growth weight from 25 to 70 changes the leader. Make the change and explain the result using the contribution and sensitivity information.
2. Leave growth weight blank, explain the displayed result and recover. Reset, then compare Florida at setup ceilings of $299,999.99 and $300,000 while holding other priorities fixed. Explain its status.
3. Reset and give growth zero weight. Inspect Louisiana and compare it with Mississippi and West Virginia. Explain whether you agree with the treatment of missing information.
4. Use the map and equivalent selector/table to inspect a small state. Explain whether an unchanged map shade before and after an edit guarantees an unchanged score. Copy the rationale and name one inference it cannot support.
