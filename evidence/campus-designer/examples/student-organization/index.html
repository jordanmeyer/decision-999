Our purpose

Better products start with better questions.

This fictional club brings together MBA students interested in the intersection of AI, people, and product management. The idea is simple: learn together, experiment thoughtfully, and make room for different perspectives.

You don’t need to code to contribute. Bring your curiosity, your experience, and a willingness to challenge the first answer.

Put curiosity into practice

Three ways we learn.

Less watching from the sidelines.
More trying things together.

01 / BUILD

Small experiments. Real questions.

Work through a product problem in a guided prototype lab. Sketch a concept, test an assumption, and share what didn’t work.

Sample format · 90-minute workshop

02 / DISCUSS

Look beyond the launch.

Pull apart an AI product decision with peers. Consider user needs, business trade-offs, and the people affected along the way.

Sample format · 60-minute case circle

03 / CONNECT

Find your next thought partner.

Meet people exploring similar questions. Practice a product interview, compare learning paths, or find a partner for a weekend build.

A few good questions.

Do I need a technical background?

No. The proposed activities welcome business, design, and technical perspectives. Workshop prompts would include a starting point for people new to AI.

What would being a member involve?

Choose the sessions that match your interests and time. The sample model has no attendance minimum; members could also help shape a case discussion or facilitate a peer session.

Can I join this organization now?

No. This is a fictional website example, not an active student organization. The event and membership controls demonstrate how a future site might work; they do not register you or reserve a place.
