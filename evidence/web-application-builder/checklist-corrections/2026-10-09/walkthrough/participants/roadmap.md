# Launch Ledger — participant task

[Open the app](https://jordanmeyer.github.io/bab-example-roadmap/). Reload or reset it before starting.

Allow 10 minutes. Start from the default app supplied by the facilitator. Think aloud; this reviews the app, not you. State predictions before changes or worked answers. You may use the app's guidance. Stop at the time limit and describe where you reached.

1. Explain the starting launch finish, promise buffer and capacity warning. Predict the finish if Packaging takes five additional days. Edit that task, apply the change and reconcile the result.
2. Leave an existing task's duration blank. Explain the displayed result, then recover a valid applied plan. Find the same task in the dates table and open its editor again.
3. Restore the original example. Make Design sign-off unavailable until December 4, 2026. Predict what must wait and what Ready to launch will mean. Apply the change and use the dependencies to explain the result.
4. Name one limitation of the dates. Explain whether changing the promised date or adding capacity automatically moves dependency-scheduled work. Describe what you would save to preserve your decision context.
