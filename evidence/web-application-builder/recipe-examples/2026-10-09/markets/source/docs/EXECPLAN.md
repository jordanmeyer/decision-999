## Surprises & Discoveries

Leaflet remains candidate in the inventory; maintainer authorizes this example but the approved-only checker must report its rejection honestly. US Census Generalized_ACS2024 States20M endpoint provides GeoJSON directly, so no geometry-conversion dependency is needed. Its geometry is statistical and generalized, not a legal or address-level boundary. Visually hidden absolute spans in wide table cells can escape scroll clipping when no positioned ancestor contains them; anchoring them to their buttons fixed observed page overflow.
