The builders

Before business school, the builders ran diligence, managed supply chains, sized markets, and reconciled budgets. They know which numbers matter, where AI slips, and when a person needs to step in. The course turns that know-how into plugins.

The course

${name} is an MBA course in which students turn problems from their own careers into tested plugins, using only public or synthetic data. The strongest are published here at the end of each term.

The process

How every plugin is made

  1. Starts with a real problem

    A task its builder knows firsthand, measured first: how long it takes and how often it goes wrong.

  2. Grounded in sources

    Every number and claim traces to its source. Without evidence, the plugin says so instead of guessing.

  3. Tested and reviewed

    Tested against multiple cases and thoroughly reviewed by MBA students.

  4. Published with its limits

    Only plugins that pass review are listed, with their results, where they fail, and where a person should approve the work.
