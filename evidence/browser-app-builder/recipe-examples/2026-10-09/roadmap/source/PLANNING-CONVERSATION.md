# Simulated planning conversation

These are role-play exchanges with the coordinating agent as a fictional student, not claims about a real student or customer. Preserved verbatim, including compact spacing in messages.

Student opening: “I want a realistic roadmap/project-plan app.” Coordinator requested actual planning questions before model choices.

Developer asked which fictional launch decision the tool should support; calendar-day versus workday convention; whether dependencies calculate dates or bars are freely drawn; baseline comparison and import/export needs. These initial questions are summarized because their exact earlier wording is not retained in this continuation; no invented verbatim transcript is claimed.

Student: “I’m leading a fictional consumer-products team launching a refillable desk cleaner. I want a dependency-driven launch schedule that shows whether our promised launch date still holds when packaging or safety testing slips. I want dependencies to calculate the earliest dates rather than silently let me draw impossible bars. A misleading plan would show launch before testing clears or hide an upstream delay. Show baseline versus revised dates and critical tasks, with local JSON export/import. We have product validation, packaging, supplier readiness, pilot batch, safety test, sales materials, and launch workstreams. Ask me to confirm your proposed realistic task durations/dependencies and target date before building.”
