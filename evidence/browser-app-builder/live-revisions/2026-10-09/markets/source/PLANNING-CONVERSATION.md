Developer: “One implementation clarification while I prepare geometry: to make 'a pinned state becomes excluded' a real interaction, I propose editable delivery/setup ceilings (defaults$12/order and$250k), with fixed scoring anchors remaining unchanged. Implementation interpretation: complete excluded markets may show their illustrative component score with an exclusion marker for comparison, but never receive a rank or enter automatic top3. Incomplete markets never get a total score. This makes the before-ranking exclusion and pinned comparison meaningful without treating an exclusion as score0.
