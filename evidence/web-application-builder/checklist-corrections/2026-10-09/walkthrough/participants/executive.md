# Stillwater Coffee — participant task

[Open the app](https://jordanmeyer.github.io/bab-example-executive/). Reload or reset it before starting.

Allow 8 minutes. Start from the default app supplied by the facilitator. Think aloud; this reviews the app, not you. State predictions before changing inputs or opening worked answers. You may use the app's guidance. Stop at the time limit and describe where you reached.

1. Describe the current company financial picture and the comparison periods. What does contribution include or exclude?
2. Predict what increasing the margin target from 20% to 25% will change. Make the change and explain what changed and what stayed the same.
3. Enter 101% as the target. Explain the feedback, recover a valid target and identify which results remain meaningful while the target is invalid.
4. Open the board briefing. Before revealing its interpretation, predict Harbor Wharf's main issue from the chart and table. Inspect the full cost detail, return to the question and propose the next evidence you would request.
5. Record and copy a takeaway with evidence, a next investigation and one limitation on a staffing or expansion recommendation. Explain whether your notes survive reload.
