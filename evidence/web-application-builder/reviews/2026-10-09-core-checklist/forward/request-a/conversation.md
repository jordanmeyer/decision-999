This is a **simulated** student conversation for a forward exercise on 2026-10-09. Replies are synthetic domain choices, not evidence of a real interview, approval, organization or outcome. No app was built.

Finance alone needs `480 × 0.30 × 25 = 3,600` minutes each week, more than its 3,150 available. The 471.5-minute arithmetic cannot establish sustainable turnaround.
