# Tournament Atlas (bab-example-madness): fidelity checklist

Review of https://jordanmeyer.github.io/bab-example-madness/ (source `6f98ed0`) against the archived FiveThirtyEight 2015 men's bracket, done on October 9, 2026. Both pages were exercised side by side in a browser at 1024, 1217, 1280 and 1680px and at 375px. Every year from 2016 to 2026 was scanned for clipped labels, and the original's bundled `app.js` was read to confirm behavior rather than infer it from screenshots. P1 items break the visualization behavior the brief asked to match, P2 items are visible defects, and P3 items are polish.

The execution agent's reviews passed because they checked one year (2023) at one width (1280×720). Most of the items below show up as soon as you change the year, the window width, the snapshot or the mode. From now on, acceptance needs to cover all years, at least three desktop widths, a late snapshot and Results.

## P1 · Behavior that doesn't match the original

- [ ] **Stop drawing completed rounds as full-width bands with "100%" labels.** In a late snapshot, Houston's route draws 15.5px navy bands with "100%" labels through every round it has already won. The labels sit on top of the Houston name strips, and the strips merge into the band. The original (`showPath`) starts the route at the furthest slot the team has reached, and it gives filled slots no percentage label (labels are only created for nodes with no team). Completed rounds are shown only by the team's highlighted name strips. Fix: in `decorateRoute` (`app/main.js:168`), skip stages where `p === 1` for both the route and the label, and begin at the first unresolved stage.
- [ ] **Make the whole bracket respond to the pointer.** In the original, a Voronoi overlay over every node means any point in the bracket previews the nearest slot. Here, only the 127 small buttons respond. Moving into the space between them leaves the previous team's route on screen. That was confirmed: the pointer was over empty West/Midwest space and the bracket still showed Alabama. Fix: one `pointermove` listener on `.bracket-tree` that finds the nearest precomputed slot center (127 points, brute force is fine). Keep the buttons for keyboard use only.
- [ ] **Start every year from a clean bracket.** `loadYear` pins the title favorite (`app/main.js:56`), so the first screen always shows Houston's route, and leaving a preview returns to that route. In the original, the resting state has no route, and the center shows only "Hover over each slot to see the team most likely to get there" (`unselectTeam`). Fix: start with `selectedId = null`, show the instruction in the center, pin a team only on an explicit click or Enter, and clear it with Escape.
- [ ] **Fix clipped team names in 9 of 10 years.** At 1280px these labels are cut off with an ellipsis: 2016 (`Vanderbilt / Wichita St.`, `Stephen F. Austin`, `Arkansas-Little Rock`, `Middle Tennessee`, `FDU / Florida Gulf Coast`), 2017 (`Mount St. Mary's / New Orleans`, `Wake Forest / Kansas St.`), 2018 (`Radford / LIU Brooklyn`, `St. Bonaventure / UCLA`), 2019, 2021, 2022, 2024, 2025 and 2026. At 1217px, 2023 clips too (`Northweste…`). The two abbreviation maps at `app/main.js:121` and `:128` were hand-tuned for the one year that was reviewed. Fix: generate a `bracketName` for each team in data preparation, as the original did with its `short_name`/`super_short_name` fields. Add a check that measures every label in every year at the narrowest bracket width.
- [ ] **Fix the table's heat scale, which reads in the wrong order.** The bins are gray (<10%), pale blue `rgb(226,230,237)` (10–25%), cream `rgb(252,247,229)` (25–50%), yellow (≥50%) and navy (✓). Cream is lighter than pale blue, so 41% looks weaker than 22%. The original uses one continuous single-hue scale (`d3.scale.linear` from white to one color) with light text at ≥50%. Fix: interpolate one Duke hue by `p` (`style.css:116–121`, `app/main.js` `renderTable`). Keep navy only for ✓ if needed.

## P2 · Visible defects

- [ ] **Stop stretching the SVG.** `preserveAspectRatio="none"` with a 1200-unit viewBox, combined with HTML nodes placed by percentage, scales the bracket horizontally only. At 1680px the x-scale is 1.28 and the y-scale is 1.0, so probability labels are stretched 28% wide and the horizontal segments get long and flat. At the 1100px minimum, labels are squeezed to 92%. The original is a fixed 1004px drawing. Fix: give the bracket a fixed width and center it (or scale it uniformly), and drop `preserveAspectRatio="none"`.
- [ ] **Make the highlighted name strip contrast with the route.** Highlighted strips turn navy (`style.css:81`), the same color as the route, so the strip, the band and the label run together. The original switches the team's strips to a light fill (`rgb(241,242,242)`) with bold dark text, so the name stays readable against a dark route.
- [ ] **Put the snapshot status inside the bracket.** The original shows "Men's / Predictions through March 22 games" (date in the accent color) at the top center of the bracket. The recreation puts it in the timeline panel, which is out of view once you scroll to the bracket (the tree is 1,024px tall). The page header, year field, toolbar and timeline also push the first bracket row to about y500. Fix: move the descriptor into the bracket center, and merge the year, mode and view controls into one row above the timeline.
- [ ] **Animate the route.** In the original, the route grows one stage at a time (`transition().duration(50 + stage × 100)`) and shrinks back the same way on mouseout. Here it switches instantly, which loses the "trace the path" feel. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- [ ] **Results mode: one identity, and per-game losses.** With Houston pinned, Results draws Houston's route while the center says "Connecticut · Winner". The `eliminated` rule (`app/main.js:198`, `p[6] === 0`) also greys every team except the champion, so the bracket no longer shows who won each game. Fix: the center should follow the previewed team (champion or "eliminated in Sweet 16"). Grey a strip only where that team lost from that slot (`p[round + 1] === 0`), in both modes.
- [ ] **Phone table: put the answer first.** At 375px, the first table row is at y760 of an 812px screen. The scroll area is 826px wide inside a 341px box, and Region, Seed and an all-✓ Round of 64 column come before any probability, with Win title last. The original's phone table had only a short name and the seven round columns. Fix: on narrow screens, drop Region and Seed (fold the seed into the name), hide the Round of 64 column when every team in it has reached it, and make Win title the second column.

## P3 · Polish

- [ ] **First Four slots:** draw unresolved play-in strips in grey with dark text, as the original does (`rect.playin`). While a slot is previewed, show the predicted winner's name instead of `SEMO / TAMUCC`; at the moment the center names one team while the strip still shows both.
- [ ] **Emphasize the hovered stage's probability.** The original bolds and enlarges the label for the depth you're pointing at (`.selected-percentage`, 700 18px against 16px).
- [ ] **Show the title-game date on the timeline as unavailable.** For 2023, the axis ends at Apr 1, and the Apr 3 final isn't marked. The original shows the future date as a grey tick ("6").
- [ ] **Play at the original's pace and pause hover during playback.** 1,400ms per step against 500ms makes an 11-step replay take 15s; the original also turns off Voronoi hover while it plays.
- [ ] **Simplify the date control to one control.** A native range, a row of date buttons and, on narrow screens, a select all set the same value. Use one track whose ticks are the available dates, with arrow keys moving between them, and the round name under the selected tick as in the original ("3rd round"). Derive whether a snapshot includes that day's games from the data instead of hedging with "may include".
- [ ] **Revisit the 1100px breakpoint.** The original's bracket fits in 1004px, but the recreation shows the table first at 1024px. The view is also chosen once at load (`app/main.js:13`), so widening the window never brings the bracket back.
- [ ] **Raise text below 10px:** seeds in the bracket strips (`.54rem`, 8.6px), the snapshot phase (9.9px), the timeline eyebrow (9.4px), stage-strip labels (9.1px, and 8.3px on phones). Team names at 10.6px are also smaller than the original's 12px.
- [ ] **Make the bracket one tab stop.** It currently takes 127 Tab presses to get past the bracket. Use a roving tabindex: arrow keys move between rounds and slots, and Tab leaves the bracket.
- [ ] **Show that a click pinned the team.** The detail panel is about 1,000px below the bracket, so clicking a slot looks like nothing happened. Add a small "Pinned: Houston ×" chip next to the bracket.
- [ ] **Clean up `decorateRoute`.** It runs on every `pointerover`, including moving between the spans inside one button, and it rewrites `title`/`aria-label` on all 127 nodes each time. Return early when the id hasn't changed, and build the labels once per render.

## Deliberate differences: keep them

No team logos, one navy route instead of team colors, men's tournament only, the separate Results mode, keyboard focus preview, the sticky team column, and labeled reconstructions for 2024–2026. Don't undo these while working through the list above.
