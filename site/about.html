The builders

The hard part of using AI at work isn’t the model. It’s knowing what good work looks like.

Before business school, the builders ran diligence, managed supply chains, sized markets, and reconciled budgets. They know which numbers matter, where analysts slip, and when a person needs to step in. The course turns that know-how into plugins.

Quality

How every plugin is made

  1. Starts with a real problem

    Each plugin targets a task its builder knows firsthand, measured first: how long it takes today and how often it goes wrong.

  2. Grounded in sources

    Every number and claim traces back to where it came from. When the evidence isn’t there, the plugin says so instead of guessing.

  3. Tested and attacked

    Each plugin runs against dozens of test cases with known answers for accuracy, consistency, and cost. Then classmates try to break it.

  4. Published with its limits

    Only plugins that pass review are listed here, with their results, where they fail, and where a person should approve the work.
