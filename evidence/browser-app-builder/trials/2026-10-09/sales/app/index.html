CSV format & limits

Exact headers: region,product,quantity,unit_price,unit_cost. USD prices/costs up to $1,000,000, at most 2 decimal places; whole quantities from 0–1,000,000. Region/product labels: 1–120 characters. Up to 5,000 rows and 2 MB. Invalid files are rejected completely; current data stays intact.
