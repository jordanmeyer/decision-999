# Resume

Read [the conversation rules](../../references/conversation.md) and [the design rules](../../references/design.md). Run `course start resume`.

## Ask

1. What role are you seeking, and who should be impressed by this site?
2. Which two or three experiences should lead, and what evidence supports each?
3. Which contact details may be public? Tell the student: "Anything published stays in the history permanently."
4. Which work samples are you allowed to share?
5. What one sentence should headline the page?

## Run

1. Build the site in `resume`: the headline, the leading experiences with their evidence, the samples and the contact details, in the student's words. Put the name and headline in `app/site.json`.
2. Show the preview and ask: "What would the person you named in the first answer change?" Make those changes.
3. Write the README and end the session.

## The student sees

"Your resume is live at <address>. Look at it on your phone too."
