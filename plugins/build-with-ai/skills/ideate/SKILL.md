# Ideate

Read [the conversation rules](../../references/conversation.md) and [what fits](../../references/scope.md).

## Ask, for each of two ideas

1. What recurring task or decision have you seen first-hand?
2. Who does it, and how often?
3. What does it cost today, in time, money or mistakes?
4. What data would it need, and could you share it?
5. What should the app give that person, and when?
6. How would you know it helped, in terms someone could observe?
7. Say whether the idea fits a browser app, and why. If not, ask: "How could it be reshaped to fit?"

When the student is stuck, tell them two examples from the project list and ask: "Which is closest to something you have seen?"

## Run

1. Save each idea as `ideas/<short-name>.md` with four headings: User, Task, Intended output, Success criteria, in the student's words.
2. Write the journal entry and pipe it to `course journal ideas --skill ideate`; there is nothing to check or publish.

## The student sees

"Your two ideas are saved in the ideas folder. Submit both summaries."
