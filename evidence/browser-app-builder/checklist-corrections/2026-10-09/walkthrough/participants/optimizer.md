# Batch & Balance — participant task

[Open the app](https://jordanmeyer.github.io/bab-example-optimizer/). Reload or reset it before starting.

Allow 10 minutes. Start from the default app supplied by the facilitator. Think aloud; this reviews the app, not you. State predictions before changes or worked answers. You may use the app's guidance. Stop at the time limit and describe where you reached.

1. Explain the opening whole-batch allocation and fractional comparison.
2. Predict whether making as many of the highest-contribution product as possible must produce the best feasible plan. Use the manual checker to compare a mix you choose with the recommendation. Explain what you learn.
3. Predict what happens if oven capacity becomes 157 minutes. Edit it and solve. Explain the result and find the minimum oven time required without changing commitments.
4. Leave a capacity blank, attempt to solve and recover the original valid case. Explain how you know the displayed allocation is current.
5. Copy the rationale. Name one production fact needed before treating this allocation as a workable timed schedule.
