# Agreed sales dashboard

An MBA student uses synthetic sales to identify regional/product results worth investigating. This does not establish causes or predict future performance. Simulation questions and verbatim agreement are in TRIAL.md.

Import a transient local CSV or immediately explore three bundled synthetic rows. Exact five headers, any order: region,product,quantity,unit_price,unit_cost. Labels trimmed, nonempty, at most 120 characters. Quantity is a nonnegative whole number up to 1,000,000. Prices/costs are nonnegative USD decimals with at most two decimal places, up to $1,000,000 each. Reject extra precision, exponent notation, currency signs, negative input, missing/extra/duplicate fields, malformed quotes, empty datasets, more than 5,000 rows or 2,000,000 bytes. Whole-file validation precedes replacement. Reject totals beyond safe integer cents. Files stay in memory; reload restores example. Nothing uploads or persists.

Compute integer cents: row revenue=quantity×unit_price cents; cost=quantity×unit_cost cents; contribution before overhead=revenue−cost. Sum matching rows. Display USD with two decimal places, no intermediate rounding. Negative contribution is valid when cost exceeds price. No taxes, overhead, returns, inventory or currency conversion. No margin percentage or causal/future-performance claims.

Independent example: North Notebook 10×$20=$200 revenue, 10×$12=$120 cost, $80 contribution; South Pen 5×$30=$150, 5×$18=$90, $60; North Notebook 2×$20=$40, 2×$12=$24, $16. Totals $390/$234/$156; North $240/$144/$96; South $150/$90/$60. North+Pen matches no rows, all totals zero. Duplicate sale rows are additive records, not deduplicated.

Include region/product intersection filters, reset filters, restore example, sortable rows, a revenue/cost/contribution bar chart and equivalent labeled numerical totals. Controls are native and labeled. Sort control outside grid supplies keyboard alternative. Empty match status is explicit. Import failure leaves current rows and filters intact. No export, editing, storage, login, network service, date grouping or backend.

Selected managed libraries: papa-parse 5.7.0 for quoted CSV, tabulator-tables 6.6.1 for substantial sortable row inspection, echarts 6.1.0 for comparison. Native filter/reduce owns calculations; Arquero unnecessary. Remaining inventory libraries serve no requirement. Pinned Vite tooling, local tokens/adapters, no CDN assets. Georgia headings / Arial body are local substitutions. Duke blues are a visual default, no logos or endorsement.

Acceptance: known totals and intersections; zero/negative-result boundaries; quoted comma/escaped quote/BOM/CRLF; malformed/missing/extra/empty/oversized inputs; text-safe HTML-like imports; transactional failure; row sorting; 5,000-record calculation; keyboard filter/sort/reset; desktop and 320px reflow with internal table scroll; chart hidden-to-visible resize and solid interactive states; no console errors; observed same-origin production resources; production /fresh-sales/ and license link. Student concerns about overhead, inventory and differing samples appear in interface. No remaining material questions. Deployment is pending authorization and destination, with workflow prepared locally only.
