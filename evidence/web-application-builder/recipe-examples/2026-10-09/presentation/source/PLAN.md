# Desk / Day — agreed presentation plan

Build an original six-slide analytical pitch for a fictional reusable desk-kit pop-up. Classmates acting as a small investment committee decide whether price and volume can cover event costs. All figures are synthetic; “live” means local interaction, with no external updates or probabilistic demand claims. The recommendation is conditional on assumptions and next validation steps.

The six-slide arc is the conditional pitch, fixed baseline economics, interactive four-input laboratory, quantity sensitivity, fixed scenario comparison plus current assumptions, and a copyable decision record. Reveal.js controls navigation/progress/keyboard. Local SVG charts have exact table alternatives. Speaker/context notes are available without a remote plugin. Mobile content remains unscaled and scrollable. No PowerPoint export, AI content, remote assets, backend or complex simulation.

Baseline: price$20, unit variable cost$12, quantity100, fixed cost$500. Revenue$2,000, variable cost$1,200, operating result$300, contribution$8/unit and whole-unit break-even63. Downside50 units gives−$100. Price$18 at100 units gives$100 and break-even84. These three reference cases remain fixed and distinct from the current assumptions.

Store money in integer cents. Accept price/cost$0–$1,000 with at most two decimal places, quantity0–10,000 whole units, fixed cost$0–$100,000. Profit = quantity×(price−unit variable cost)−fixed cost. Positive contribution gives ceil(fixed/contribution); fixed0 is a minimum non-loss quantity of0. With zero contribution and zero fixed cost, every volume exactly breaks even. With negative contribution and zero fixed cost, only zero volume avoids loss. Nonpositive contribution with positive fixed cost has no finite break-even. A finite threshold beyond the supported quantity range is identified honestly.

Inputs apply atomically; invalid/pending edits preserve prior valid calculations with an explicit label and disable decision copy until applied or restored. Presets replace all four fields. Sensitivity adapts to entered quantity, includes zero/current and up to two or more larger quantities where feasible, all bounded0–10,000 and deduplicated. At baseline the points are0/25/50/75/100/125/150/200. No probabilities are implied. The decision record includes current inputs, status, arithmetic, assumptions and next validation tests.

Use exact approved reveal.js6.0.2 and Vite8.3.4, Node22.19.0/npm10.9.3, managed build and Campus Designer with system Georgia/Arial and unchanged navy/royal. No institutional mark or endorsement.
