Read the assumptions first

Daily demand is independent and uniformly distributed between the chosen integer bounds. This is a simplifying assumption, not a demand forecast. A single run does not establish which policy is best.

Arrivals occur before demand. Unmet demand is lost, never backordered. After demand, daily reorder places one fixed order when stock plus pending orders is at or below its reorder point. Weekly review orders up to its target at the end of days 7, 14, 21… An order placed on day d arrives on day d + lead time.

Fill rate = fulfilled units ÷ requested units. Mean stock averages end-of-day stock; it excludes goods in transit. Costs, capacity constraints, seasonality and supplier uncertainty are outside this model.
